### Updating
Install over the old version. You don't need to uninstall first.
- **Windows:** run `RickWire-Setup-0.2.9.exe`. Settings, keys, phones and queue are kept.
- **macOS:** drag the app into Applications and choose *Replace*.
- **Linux:** unpack over the old folder.

Phones keep working; refresh the RickWire tab once.

The Mac version is still untested on real hardware. Please report what you find.
